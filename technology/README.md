# Technology

## APIs and Integrations

- [Brasil API](https://brasilapi.com.br/docs?utm_source=chatgpt.com): Open-source project providing free APIs for querying Brazilian information, such as postal codes (CEP), company registration numbers (CNPJ), banks, public holidays, and other useful data for application integration.
- [Government API Catalog](https://www.gov.br/conecta/catalogo/?utm_source=chatgpt.com): Official catalog of Brazilian government APIs, bringing together documented services and endpoints for integration and information sharing between public systems and agencies.

## File Conversion and Processing

- [Api2Convert](https://www.api2convert.com/documentation?utm_source=chatgpt.com): API for converting, compressing, and processing files in various formats, including documents, images, videos, and audio.
- [I Love API](https://www.iloveapi.com/docs/api-reference?utm_source=chatgpt.com): Set of APIs for integrating PDF document and image processing capabilities into applications, including file conversion, compression, splitting, merging, and editing.

## Open Data and Databases

- [Base dos Dados](https://basedosdados.org/search?utm_source=chatgpt.com): Platform that brings together organized and processed public datasets, facilitating queries, data cross-referencing, and analysis of Brazil's socioeconomic information.
- [BCB Open Data](https://dadosabertos.bcb.gov.br/dataset?utm_source=chatgpt.com): Portal of the Central Bank of Brazil providing public datasets on the economy, financial system, credit, foreign exchange, Pix, financial institutions, and economic indicators.

- [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/products?utm_source=chatgpt.com): Community of independent entrepreneurs and digital product creators, offering a space to discover projects, learn from other founders' experiences, and follow online businesses.
- [Micro SaaS - SaaS Directory](https://diretorio.microsaas.com.br/?utm_source=chatgpt.com): Brazilian directory of Micro SaaS products and digital tools, allowing users to discover products, meet entrepreneurs, and explore independent business opportunities.
- [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com): Platform for discovering and launching new technology products, allowing users to follow the latest releases, tools, startups, and digital solutions.
